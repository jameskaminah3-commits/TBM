# Custom requests

When nothing listed fits, or fits your budget, our team can find it for you: a stay in your price range, a photographer, a boat charter, a restaurant booking, a safari, a wedding or retreat, or a whole trip planned around your budget.

## How it works

Tell us what you need, when and where, how many people, and your budget if you have one. There's a small request fee, credited in full against the final price if you go ahead. The team reviews the request and sends a quotation, usually within 24 hours; the final price always comes from the team.

## A request or a full proposal

Most requests are a quick introduction the team arranges directly. A multi-day or multi-stop trip gets a written proposal, which takes more research.
