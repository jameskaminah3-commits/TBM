# Stays

Villas, apartments, studios and beach houses across the Coast. Each listing's page has its photos, amenities, house rules and a book button.

## Good to know

- Availability is checked live for your dates before you book.
- Every stay has a maximum number of guests.
- A stay can be combined with services in one booking: a chef, transport, errands or MamaCare.
- Check-in and check-out dates must be in the future, with check-out after check-in.
