# How booking and payment work

## How do I book?

Tell us what you'd like and when. Once you've chosen, we create your booking and send a payment link. Your booking and payment link are saved in My Bookings: sign in with your TBM account, or create one using the same email address you gave for the booking, and the booking appears there. If you forget your password, use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page.

## When are my dates reserved?

Dates are reserved once the deposit, or the full payment, is paid. There's no holding dates without payment. When you tap "Pay now", the dates are held for you for a few minutes while you pay; if another guest pays for the same dates first, the site tells you before you're charged.

## Deposit or full payment?

Stays and standard service bookings can be secured with a deposit; the amount is shown with your booking total. Custom requests and listing verifications are paid in full.

## How can I pay?

By card (Visa or Mastercard) through Paystack, and by M-Pesa; some bookings use Pesapal. M-Pesa is only in Kenyan shillings; cards can be charged in shillings or US dollars depending on the provider.

## What if my card payment doesn't work?

Contact us and we'll help: WhatsApp or call +254 718 475 264. M-Pesa is available as a fallback: after sending, share the M-Pesa transaction code in the chat so we can match it to your booking.

## Which currency will I see?

Prices are shown in US dollars or Kenyan shillings, whichever you've chosen on the site.

## How far ahead do I need to book?

Bookings through the chat need at least a day's notice: stays need 24 hours, chefs and experiences 12 hours, and cars, errands and MamaCare 6 hours (always on a later calendar day). Same-day requests go to our team directly. Dates are in Kenya time.

## How many people can a booking be for?

Up to 12 guests for stays and experiences, and up to 20 for chefs. For bigger groups, ask for a custom request.
