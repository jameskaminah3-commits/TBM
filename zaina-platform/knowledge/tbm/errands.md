# Errands, shopping, laundry and cleaning

## Shopping

We shop for you: groceries, supplies, anything on your list. Tell us your list and a budget for the shopping itself; the service fee is on top.

## Laundry

Priced by weight, with optional extras such as ironing.

## House cleaning

Priced by the number of bedrooms, with optional extras.

## Other errands

A flat-fee errand for anything else you need picked up or done.

## What we need

Where the service is (your accommodation or address), the date, and the detail that sets the price: your shopping budget, the laundry weight, or the number of bedrooms.
