# Listing verification

Found a villa on Airbnb, Facebook, Instagram or Jiji, or did an agent send you a place on WhatsApp? Before you pay someone you don't know, our team visits it for you: we check the property exists, matches its advert, and flag anything that looks wrong. It works for car-hire and tour adverts too.

## What the report covers

- Photos of every room and the outside
- Whether the property exists and matches its listing
- The neighbourhood's safety
- Water, power and internet
- How far it is to the nearest beach, shops and hospital
- Any discrepancies or red flags seen during the visit

You get a written report with photos within 72 hours of the visit, with either a verified outcome or a warning flag.

## Where we verify

Mombasa Island, Nyali, Bamburi, Shanzu, Mtwapa, Diani, Watamu and Malindi.

## What we need

The link to the advert, or if you don't have one, what you know: most useful is the agent's or host's phone number (or their Instagram or Facebook page), then the property's name and area, the price, and what was promised. Also tell us what you'd like checked.

## Payment

The verification fee is paid in full before anyone is sent out. If you then book with us, the fee is credited to your booking.

## What it can't promise

The visit shows the property as it is on the day of the visit. It can't guarantee how a host behaves later.
