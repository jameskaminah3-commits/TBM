# Private chefs

A private chef cooks at your accommodation, from an intimate dinner to hosting a group.

## How chefs charge

- Per plate: best for more than four guests; a minimum of four plates.
- Single meal: a flat price for one meal, with no plate minimum; good for an intimate dinner.
- Session: a few chefs charge per session instead.

Each chef sets their own prices, and we quote them exactly.

## Groceries

Ingredients are separate from the chef's service: you buy them, or we shop for them through our Errands service and bill them at cost.

## Guest numbers

Chefs have minimum and maximum guest numbers, up to 20 guests.

## Restaurant reservations

We can also request a table at a restaurant: tell us the restaurant or area, date, time, party size and any occasion or dietary needs, and the team confirms the reservation.
