# MamaCare childcare and family support

MamaCare is our trusted childcare, elderly-care and family support service. Vetted caregivers come to your accommodation to look after infants, toddlers, children or elderly family members, so parents can rest, go out or get some work done.

## Care options

- Daytime, by the hour (at least 3 hours)
- Evening, by the hour
- Overnight
- Full day

## Age groups

Infants (0 to 12 months), toddlers (1 to 3 years) and children (4 to 12 years). Prices depend on the age group and the type of care.

## What we'll ask

How many children and their ages; the dates and times you need care; any allergies, medical needs or routines; and any safety or dietary requirements.
