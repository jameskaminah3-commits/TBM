# Coast areas guide

## Nyali

Mombasa's north coast. An upscale, family-friendly suburb with malls and restaurants close by. Good for families, long stays and first-time family trips. About 20 to 30 minutes from the airport.

## Bamburi

Mombasa's north coast. Mid-range and easy to get around from, a central base. Good for budget-conscious travellers.

## Shanzu

Mombasa's north coast. A resort strip for beach-focused trips and all-inclusive resorts.

## Mtwapa

North of Mombasa. The Coast's nightlife hub and budget-friendly. Good for younger travellers, nightlife and budget stays.

## Mombasa Island

The historic city centre: Fort Jesus, the Old Town, culture and day trips.

## Diani

The south coast's white-sand beach destination, with a mix of backpacker and luxury stays. Good for beach holidays, diving, romantic getaways and first-time visitors. Getting there from Mombasa usually means the Likoni ferry.

## Malindi

Further up the north coast. Historic, with a strong Italian influence: Italian food, historic sites and a quieter pace.

## Watamu

Near Malindi. Upscale and quiet, with a marine park. Good for snorkelling, diving, eco-luxury and romantic getaways.

## Mambrui

Near Malindi. Very quiet and rural, with sand dunes: off the beaten path, for solitude.

## Which area suits you

- Nightlife: Mtwapa.
- Quiet and romantic: Watamu, Mambrui or Diani.
- First family trip: Nyali or Diani.
- Beach-focused: Diani, Watamu or Shanzu.
- Culture and history: Mombasa Island or Malindi.
- Diving and snorkelling: Watamu or Diani.
- Off the beaten path: Mambrui or Malindi.
- Budget-friendly: Bamburi or Mtwapa.
- Luxury: Watamu, Nyali or Diani.
- Italian food and atmosphere: Malindi.
