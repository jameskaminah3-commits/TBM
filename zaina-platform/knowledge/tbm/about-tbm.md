# About Tembea Bila Matata

Tembea Bila Matata ("travel without the hassle") is a coastal hospitality, travel and concierge platform for the Kenyan Coast. It brings stays, private chefs, transport, errands, MamaCare childcare and experiences together in one place, so guests don't have to juggle WhatsApp chats with ten different vendors.

## Who it's for

Visitors and residents alike: holidaymakers, business travellers, families, digital nomads, expats and local families. Most services can be booked on their own, without booking a stay.

## What makes it different

- Real local knowledge: the team lives and works on the Coast.
- One place coordinates every part of a trip.
- Transparent pricing, with no hidden commissions passed to guests.
- Signature service: on-the-ground verification of listings found elsewhere (Airbnb, Facebook, Jiji, agents).
- Signature service: MamaCare, trusted childcare and family support.

## Where we operate

Mombasa, Nyali, Bamburi, Shanzu, Mtwapa, Diani, Watamu, Malindi and the wider Kenyan Coast.
