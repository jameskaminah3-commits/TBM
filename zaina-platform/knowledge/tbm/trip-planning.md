# Planning a trip

## A trip within your budget

Tell us how many people, your dates and your budget, where you're coming from and where you'd like to go, and we put together a stay, transport and an experience, with the total and whether it fits your budget. If it doesn't fit, we can adjust it or have the team find options within it.

## Travelling with children

We'll ask about ages, sleeping arrangements, and whether you need a pool, beach access or a kitchen. MamaCare caregivers can look after the children so the adults get a proper break.

## Groups

For five or more people, we'll ask how many people, how many sleeping spaces, your dates and an approximate budget.

## First time on the Coast

Tell us how many days you have and what you enjoy most (beaches, food, culture, adventure, or a bit of everything), and we'll suggest where to stay and what to do.

## Already have a place to stay?

We can arrange everything around it: airport or SGR transfers, a chef, a car for your stay, experiences, errands, and MamaCare if there are children or elderly family members.
