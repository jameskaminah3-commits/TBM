# Experiences and tours

Day trips, dhow cruises, snorkelling, food and culture tours and more along the Coast.

## Private or shared

- Private: just your group, priced per person, with a minimum number of guests.
- Shared: join others on a fixed departure, priced per person, with limited places.

We check places on your date before you book.

## Something bespoke

For a tailor-made itinerary or an activity we don't list, ask for a custom request; the team usually prepares it within 24 hours.
