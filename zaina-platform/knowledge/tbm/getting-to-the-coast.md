# Getting to and around the Coast

These are general guides, not live information: journey times depend on traffic and the ferry.

## By air

Moi International Airport, Mombasa (MBA). Roughly 20 to 30 minutes to Nyali and 60 to 90 minutes to Diani, depending on the Likoni ferry.

## By train (SGR)

The Madaraka Express (SGR) runs between Nairobi and Mombasa in about 5.5 hours, in Economy and First class. Guests book SGR tickets directly with Madaraka Express; we arrange the transfer and the stay around the train times. The Mombasa terminus is at Miritini, not in the city centre: Nyali is roughly 20 km away and Diani roughly 40 km plus the Likoni ferry.

## Approximate transfer times

- Airport to Nyali: 20 to 30 minutes
- Airport to Bamburi, Shanzu or Mtwapa: 45 to 60 minutes
- Airport to Diani: 60 to 90 minutes, including the Likoni ferry
- Airport to Watamu or Malindi: 90 to 120 minutes
- SGR terminus to Nyali: 30 to 40 minutes
- SGR terminus to Diani: 90 minutes or more
- Mombasa to Watamu: about 2 hours
- Mombasa to Malindi: about 2.5 hours

## Transfers

We arrange airport and SGR transfers with a chauffeur, and car hire for getting around during the stay.
