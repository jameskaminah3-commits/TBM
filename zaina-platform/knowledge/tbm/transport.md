# Transport and car hire

## Options

- Self-drive: by the day; mileage limits may apply, and a valid driving licence is required.
- Chauffeur for the day: a car with a driver.
- Chauffeur by the hour: a minimum of 3 hours, starting and ending on the same day.

## What we need

The date or dates, the number of passengers, self-drive or chauffeur, the pickup location, and the return or drop-off location; for hourly chauffeur, the start and end times.

## Prices

Some cars have prices by zone (area). We quote the exact price for your trip, and check the car is free on your dates before you book.

## Airport and SGR transfers

We arrange pickups from Moi International Airport and the SGR terminus at Miritini, timed to your flight or train.
