# Coast travel tips

## When is the best time to visit the Coast?

December to February and July to October are the driest and most popular months. April and May are quieter and usually cheaper, but rainy. November is a shoulder month.

## What is the weather like, and when does it rain?

The Coast has two rainy seasons: the long rains, roughly April to May, and the short rains, around November. The dry seasons are December to February and July to October. For a forecast, check a weather service: we don't give forecasts.

## Is the Coast safe for tourists?

The main resort and tourist areas are generally safe. Take the usual precautions: don't walk alone at night in unlit areas, use registered taxis or our chauffeurs, and keep valuables in your accommodation's safe. For a specific safety concern, our team is happy to help directly.

## Is it safe for children and families?

Yes: Nyali and Diani are popular with families. Many stays have pools or beach access, and our MamaCare caregivers can look after children at your accommodation.

## Do I need a visa for Kenya?

Most visitors need an electronic travel authorisation (eTA) before travelling. Requirements change, so check the official Kenyan government portal for your nationality; we can't give visa advice, but our team can point you there.

## What about vaccinations, malaria or health questions?

We can't give medical advice. A travel clinic in your home country is the right place for vaccinations and malaria prevention; for anything urgent during your stay, our team can help you find care.

## How much should I tip?

Tipping is appreciated but not required: it's common for guides, drivers and villa staff, and entirely at your discretion based on the service.

## Which languages are spoken?

Swahili and English are both official languages, and most tourism staff speak English. Italian is common in Malindi, and German is heard in Watamu and Diani.

## How do people pay locally?

M-Pesa is the most common way to pay locally. Cards are accepted at larger places but not everywhere, and US dollars are accepted at many hotels but not in local shops.

## Will I have internet and mobile data?

Mobile data is widely available on the Coast; Safaricom has the best coverage. Most stays have Wi-Fi; ask us if it matters for a particular stay.
