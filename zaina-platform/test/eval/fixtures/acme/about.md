# About Acme Guesthouse

Acme Guesthouse is a family-run guesthouse with six rooms in Watamu, a five-minute walk from Watamu beach and ten minutes from the marine park. Breakfast is included with every stay.

## Where we are

Garoda Road, Watamu, Kilifi County. From Malindi airport it's about 25 minutes by car; from Mombasa about two hours.

## Facilities

A small pool, a garden, free Wi-Fi in every room, and parking for four cars. There is no gym and no restaurant on site.
