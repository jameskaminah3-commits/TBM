# House rules

## Check-in and check-out

Check-in is from 2 pm and check-out is by 10 am. Early check-in or late check-out can be arranged if the room is free.

## Pets

Small dogs are welcome in two of our garden rooms; please tell us in advance. Other pets aren't allowed.

## Quiet hours

Please keep noise down between 10 pm and 7 am. No parties.

## Smoking

Smoking is only allowed in the garden.
