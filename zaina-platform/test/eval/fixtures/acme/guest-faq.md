## Is breakfast included?

Yes, breakfast is included and served from 7 to 10 am. Dinner can be arranged on request for KSh 1,500 per person.

## Do you have a pool?

Yes, a small pool in the garden, open from 8 am to 7 pm.

## Can you arrange airport transfers?

Yes, from Malindi airport. Tell us your flight time when you book.

## How do I book?

Tell us your dates and the number of guests; the team confirms availability and sends the booking details.
